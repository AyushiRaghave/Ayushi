PRODUCT DECISION BRIEF

The concept starts from a familiar barrier: people know the outcome they want, but may not know how to express the task, context and constraints in a useful prompt.

The decision

Structure the first interaction around the intended task and the context needed to complete it. The key product test is whether this reduces effort and improves output usefulness compared with a blank prompt.
